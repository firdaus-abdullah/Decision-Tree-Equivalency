Fail & Investigate

Pair Failure

Overall Study Criterion
Mean Recovery of Cat C Pairs
Must be between 75% and 125% across 3 test days.*
OVERALL STUDY EQUIVALENCE
PRS3 is equivalent to ELx808IU if:
  • All Category A and B criteria met
  • Every Cat C pair recovery is 50–200%
  • Mean Cat C recovery is 75–125%
*If 0 Cat C pairs exist, low-level equivalence is demonstrated.
